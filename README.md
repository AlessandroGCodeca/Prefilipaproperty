A private, automated real estate deal machine for Slovakia, built entirely for personal investment use.
The core idea is that every morning it scrapes thousands of Slovak property listings, scores them on how far the price sits below the local market and what it yields, and screens their title deeds: a flat whose own LV (list vlastníctva) shows a non-bank lien, an execution, an insolvency or a lawsuit is rejected. You wake up to a curated shortlist of deals already classified and ranked — no manual searching.
The title-deed check has one limit worth knowing up front: it can only read a flat's own LV once you give it the flat's LV number (from the seller's papers or the agent). The map pin leads to the building plot, and the plot's LV is not the flat's. So almost every listing starts as UNVERIFIED — never as clean — and the LV TO-DO tab lists the GREEN and YELLOW deals waiting for a number, with bulk entry.
The financial engine is the centrepiece. It runs every property through two simultaneous 2026 tax scenarios — buying personally versus through an s.r.o. Passive rental income is exempt from health levies in both cases, so the decision turns on the tax structure itself: personal ownership pays progressive income tax (19% / 25%) with a €500 rental exemption, while an s.r.o. deducts mortgage interest and pays a low small-company corporate rate (10%) but is then hit by dividend tax (10%) when profit is distributed. The s.r.o. also pays a yearly running cost (bookkeeping, annual accounts) and borrows on company-loan terms, so it is only recommended when it still comes out ahead after those and repays its setup cost within the hold period. Tax figures are kept per tax year with a dated source note.
The dashboard is built around a live deal feed of GREEN and YELLOW properties — each with the highest price that keeps it a deal, its discount to the regional market (adjusted for room count, and per okres in Bratislava), a +2 pp rate-shock test, a 10-year IRR with exit costs, price history and days tracked. GREEN and YELLOW measure price against the market, not income: a flag marks the ones that still cost money every month, and a filter hides them. Alongside the feed there is a map, a what-if / tax toggle calculator you can run on any property in real time (rate, LTV, term, rent, personal vs s.r.o., the company's loan terms), a deal-stage pipeline, a satellite viewer with saved vibe notes, a rejected-listings log with reasons, an LV to-do queue, live rent comps from real rental listings, a one-page PDF investment memo, and a one-click notary contract draft generator.
The longer-term vision includes a subscription layer where vetted investors pay €500/month to see the GREEN and YELLOW feed, and a middle-man spread model where you assign deals to buyers for a €10–20k fee without ever owning the property yourself — turning the data engine into a revenue stream. Both would need legal advice first — scraped portal data, the unofficial cadastre scraper, personal data under GDPR and brokerage rules all apply (see "Before this becomes a product" in sovereign_final/README.md).
